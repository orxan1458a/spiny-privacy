# SPINY – Privacy Policy

*Last updated: October 19, 2026*

This Privacy Policy explains how the mobile game **SPINY** (package name `com.spiny.game`, the "Game") collects, uses and shares information. The Game is developed by **Orxan** ("we", "us").

## 1. Summary

- The Game does not require an account or login.
- We do not ask for or collect your name, e-mail address, phone number, contacts, photos or precise location.
- The Game has no real-money purchases. Coins are earned and spent only inside the Game.
- The Game shows ads from Google AdMob and uses Google Firebase to measure how the Game is played and to fix crashes. These services collect some device information as described below.

## 2. Information collected by third-party services

| Service (provider) | Information | Purpose |
|---|---|---|
| Google AdMob and User Messaging Platform (Google LLC) | Advertising ID, device and app information (model, operating system, app version, language), IP address and approximate location (country) derived from it, ad interactions | Showing ads, including rewarded ads you choose to watch; measuring ads; preventing fraud; asking for and storing your consent where required |
| Google Firebase Analytics (Google LLC) | App usage events (for example: level started or finished, throws, upgrades bought with in-game coins, ads watched), Firebase installation ID, device and app information, approximate location (country) | Understanding how the Game is played and improving it |
| Google Firebase Crashlytics (Google LLC) | Crash reports, diagnostic and performance data, device and app information, Firebase installation ID | Finding and fixing crashes and bugs |

These services are operated by Google LLC, which processes the information under its own privacy policy: <https://policies.google.com/privacy>. See also how Google uses information from apps that use its services: <https://policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites>.

## 3. Information stored on your device

Your game progress (unlocked levels, scores, coins, upgrades and settings) is stored locally on your device. It is not sent to us. Uninstalling the Game deletes this data.

## 4. How information is shared

We do not sell your personal information. Information is shared only with the service providers listed above (Google AdMob and Google Firebase) for the purposes described. Ad partners working with AdMob may receive the advertising ID and device information to show and measure ads, in line with your consent choices.

## 5. Your choices and consent

- **Ad consent:** In regions where it is required (for example the EEA, the UK and Switzerland), the Game asks for your consent before personalised ads are shown or analytics data is collected. You can change your choice at any time in the Game under *Settings › PRIVACY*.
- **Advertising ID:** You can reset your advertising ID or opt out of personalised ads in your Android device settings (*Settings › Google › Ads* or *Settings › Privacy › Ads*).
- **Stopping all collection:** You can stop all collection by uninstalling the Game.

## 6. Data retention and security

Information sent to Google services is encrypted in transit. Analytics data is kept for the retention period configured in Google Analytics (at most 14 months). Crash reports are kept for up to 90 days. Advertising data is kept as described in Google's privacy policy.

## 7. Deleting your data

Because the Game has no accounts, we cannot link analytics or advertising data to you personally. To ask us to delete data associated with your device, contact us at the address below and include your request. You can also reset your advertising ID in your device settings.

## 8. Children

The Game is not directed to children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If you believe a child has provided us with personal information, please contact us and we will delete it.

## 9. Changes to this policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. The updated version will be posted on this page with a new "Last updated" date.
